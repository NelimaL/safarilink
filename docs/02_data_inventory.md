# 2. Data Inventory

## Overview

This project combines synthetic operational data for SafariLink Transport Services with publicly available contextual data from the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS). The synthetic data will be used to demonstrate transport analytics, while the public datasets will provide demographic and economic context.

## Public Data Sources

### Consumer Price Index and Inflation

- Publisher: Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS).
- Main source: https://www.knbs.or.ke/cpi-and-inflation-rates/
- Data explorer: https://cpiexplorer.knbs.or.ke/explorer
- Planned period: January 2023 to September 2026, subject to source availability.
- Purpose: To provide economic context when examining changes in simulated bookings and ticket revenue.
- Collection record: Date:10/21/2026,  National geographic, (county_population_source and cpi_monthly)

### County Population

- Publisher: Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS).
- Source page: https://www.knbs.or.ke/reports/kenya-census-2019/
- Dataset: 2019 Kenya Population and Housing Census — population, households, and density by county.
- Reference year: 2019.
- Purpose: To compare branch locations with county population and provide demographic context.
- Collection record: Add the actual download date and original workbook name after downloading.

## Data Limitations

The operational records are synthetic and do not describe the actual performance of a real transport company. County population figures represent the 2019 census and should not be presented as current population estimates. National CPI and inflation are broad economic indicators; they do not directly measure transport-sector costs or explain changes in simulated ticket demand on their own.
