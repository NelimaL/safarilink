# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) – Conclusions and Management Recommendations

**Project: SafariLink Transport Services**

## 1. Conclusion

The exploratory data analysis examined booking trends, seasonal demand, holiday patterns, route performance, payment preferences, and customer retention using the cleaned transport dataset.

The analysis identified December as the highest-demand month, with April and August also recording strong demand. Average daily bookings were higher on public holidays and near-holiday dates than on ordinary days. Nairobi–Mombasa, Mombasa–Nairobi, and Nairobi–Kisumu were the highest-revenue routes.

M-Pesa dominated payment methods, accounting for 73.06% of bookings, followed by cash at 15.53% and card at 8.42%. The recorded customer churn rate was 27.90%, with occasional customers recording the highest segment churn rate at 28.87%. In addition, 20,621 bookings had unknown payment methods, highlighting a data quality concern.

## 2. Management Recommendations

1. **Improve seasonal capacity planning:** Prepare bus schedules, staffing, and vehicle availability ahead of December, April, and August. Assess additional services around public holidays using route-level demand and seat occupancy data.

2. **Prioritise high-revenue routes:** Monitor Nairobi–Mombasa, Mombasa–Nairobi, and Nairobi–Kisumu closely when allocating operational resources. Evaluate occupancy, fuel, maintenance, and other operating costs before expanding services, since revenue alone does not establish profitability.

3. **Strengthen payment operations:** Maintain reliable M-Pesa services, which account for 73.06% of bookings, while retaining cash and card options to accommodate different customer preferences.

4. **Improve customer retention:** Investigate the higher recorded churn among occasional customers. Consider targeted re-engagement campaigns, repeat-travel incentives, and follow-up communications, then monitor whether these interventions improve repeat bookings.

5. **Strengthen data quality controls:** Investigate the 20,621 bookings with unknown payment methods and implement validation checks at the point of booking. Continue monitoring duplicate records, fare anomalies, and missing customer information.

6. **Use performance monitoring to guide decisions:** Develop a management dashboard tracking monthly bookings, revenue by route, seat occupancy, payment methods, and customer retention. Review these indicators regularly to support timely operational decisions.

## 3. Overall Recommendation

SafariLink management should use the findings to guide seasonal planning, route resource allocation, payment operations, and customer retention initiatives. Proposed changes should be implemented incrementally and evaluated against measurable performance indicators, operating costs, and service capacity.
