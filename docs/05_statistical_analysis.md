### Hypothesis Test: M-Pesa vs Cash Fares

**Research question:** Do average fares differ between M-Pesa and cash bookings?

**Method:** Welch's independent-samples t-test, using a 5% significance level and a 95% confidence interval.

**Results:**
- Mean fare difference (M-Pesa minus cash): KES 30.15
- t-statistic: 20.9754
- p-value: 1.49 × 10⁻⁹⁷
- 95% confidence interval: KES 27.33–32.96

**Conclusion:** We reject the null hypothesis. M-Pesa bookings have a statistically higher average fare than cash bookings in this dataset. This is an association and does not establish that payment method causes higher fares.

**Limitations and checks:** The analysis uses synthetic data. Booking independence, extreme fare values and potential differences in routes, dates and service classes should be considered when interpreting the results.
